Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This page explains how SafeYatra handles your information.

Last updated: August 2026

Introduction

SafeYatra is a travel safety application designed to help tourists with location-based services, weather, navigation, emergency assistance and travel recommendations.

Location Information

SafeYatra may use your device's location to provide features such as nearby places, weather information, emergency services and navigation.

You can control location access through your device or browser settings. Some features may not work when location access is disabled.

AI Assistant

SafeYatra includes an AI travel assistant that can help with travel questions, safety advice, recommendations and location-related queries.

Messages sent to the AI assistant may be processed through third-party AI services.

Please do not share passwords, banking details, government ID numbers or other sensitive personal information with the AI assistant.

Weather & Maps

SafeYatra may use external services to provide weather information, maps, location search and navigation.

These services may process location-related information according to their own privacy policies.

Data Usage

We aim to use only the information necessary to provide SafeYatra's features.

Information may be processed while using features such as location services, AI assistance, weather and maps.

Third-Party Services

SafeYatra may use third-party services including:

  • OpenRouter – AI services
  • Open-Meteo – Weather information
  • OpenStreetMap / Nominatim – Maps and location search
  • Google Maps – Navigation and directions
  • Firebase – Application services

Third-party services have their own privacy policies and terms of use.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by SafeYatra. API credentials are intended to be kept securely on the application backend.

However, no online service can guarantee complete security of information.

Your Choices

You can choose whether to allow location access and other optional features.

You can change location permissions at any time through your device settings.
